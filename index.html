    Straight answers

    Do I need the person's permission to air them?

    No, and they do not need a wallet. Their share accrues to an address only they can claim, by posting a code from the account. If they never claim it, it stays there. CLIP is not affiliated with any platform and nobody on the channel is endorsing it.

    What stops someone buying every slot?

    Nothing, and that is the point — the slot goes to whoever pays for it, and the rundown shows exactly who bought what. Buying the whole day is expensive and completely public.

    How does someone claim?

    They post their code from the handle it was launched on. The code is derived from the handle itself with FNV-1a, so anyone can check it without trusting us. Check a handle above to see exactly what theirs would be.

    Where do the numbers on this page come from?

    Handles and avatars are read live from X. Volume and market cap come from Dexscreener, holders from the Robinhood Chain explorer. Where a number does not exist yet, the page says so instead of filling it in.

    Is the token live?

    Not yet. When it is, the contract address will appear at the top of this page and in the pinned post. Anyone posting a contract address before it appears here is scamming you.
